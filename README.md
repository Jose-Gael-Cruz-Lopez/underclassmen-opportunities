> [!IMPORTANT]
> **A community resource for college freshmen and sophomores across all majors**, plus clearly identified incoming college freshman awards. Business, healthcare, arts, humanities, education, law, agriculture, science, engineering, and technology all belong here.

Hi, I'm [Jose Cruz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josegaelcruz). I built this to help freshmen and sophomores find a starting point: an internship, a research mentor, a scholarship, or a chance to explore a career before junior year. Whatever you're studying — or still deciding to study — you deserve access to opportunities early in college.

Browse internships, fellowships, externships, research, scholarships, career programs, and campus leadership opportunities. We welcome every academic field, including accounting, consulting, medicine and public health, nursing, pharmacy, architecture, agriculture, music, journalism, theater, creative writing, social sciences, and the trades alongside STEM. Each listing targets first- or second-year college students or a clearly identified incoming college cohort. Check its major, institution, country, citizenship, coursework, and experience requirements; some programs accept all majors and others serve a specific field.

This is community-driven. If you find something, add it. Together we make the path clearer for everyone who comes after us.

---

**To contribute** — it takes 60 seconds:
 1. [Open an issue](https://github.com/Jose-Gael-Cruz-Lopez/underclassmen-opportunities/issues/new/choose) and paste the opportunity URL
 2. A maintainer approves it — details are auto-extracted from the page
 3. A maintainer adds it to the list

---

> [!WARNING]
> This list targets **college freshmen and sophomores in every major**. First- and second-year graduate or medical-school students are outside its scope. Clearly identified incoming college freshman awards appear in the relevant scholarship sections. General internships, all-year ambassador programs, professional conferences, school-age contests, and broad all-year aid belong outside the active list.
