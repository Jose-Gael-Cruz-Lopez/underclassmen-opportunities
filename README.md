> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The only curated opportunity list built for college freshmen & sophomores (typically Classes of 2029–2030 in the 2026–27 academic year), plus explicitly identified incoming college freshman awards.** Most internship boards ignore you until junior year. This one doesn't.

Hi, I'm [Jose Cruz](https://www.linkedin.com/in/josegaelcruz). I built this because I couldn't find a single resource that actually spoke to freshmen and sophomores — every list assumed you already had experience, already knew companies, already knew where to look. This one starts from zero.

Internships, fellowships, research programs, scholarships, summits — everything here targets first and second-year college students or explicitly identified incoming college cohorts. Eligibility, coursework, and experience requirements vary by program; check the details in each listing.

This is community-driven. If you find something, add it. Together we make the path clearer for everyone who comes after us.

---

**To contribute** — it takes 60 seconds:
 1. [Open an issue](https://github.com/Jose-Gael-Cruz-Lopez/underclassmen-opportunities/issues/new/choose) and paste the opportunity URL
 2. A maintainer approves it — details are auto-extracted from the page
 3. A maintainer adds it to the list

---

> [!WARNING]
> This list targets **college freshmen and sophomores**. Specifically identified incoming college freshman awards also appear in the rising-freshmen, state, HBCU, and women in tech sections. General internships, all-year ambassador programs, professional conferences, school-age contests, and broad all-year aid belong outside the active list.

---

**Last verified: October 13, 2026 (America/New_York).** All 74 listed applications have dated verification against current official application forms, recruiting data, or published current-cycle intake instructions. Entries target college freshmen/sophomores, or incoming college freshman awards in the designated categories. Notification lists and expired sessions stay in [ARCHIVE.md](ARCHIVE.md). A working information page alone does not establish that applications are open.

See the [opportunity-by-opportunity audit](audits/2026-10-13.md) and [category-expansion evidence](audits/2026-10-13-category-expansion.md) for eligibility, application checks, and archive reasons. Each distinct application appears once; related awards sharing a form are grouped. Application forms behind personal logins were verified through current official intake dates and functioning application entry points, without accessing private applicant accounts. “Date Posted” records when a listing was added here, not the employer’s publication date.
