- Shared applications are grouped: Dartmouth ERAS/FYREE, N.C. A&T’s three Honors awards, UMBC CWIT/Cyber awards, and Swinburne’s two named STEM awards each use one row. Separate SAP technical/business requisitions and Montana award records remain distinct.

Applicants must meet its full-year enrollment requirement; no unsupported incoming-freshman eligibility is asserted.
- NOAA Hollings is open, but its third-year five-year-degree and unrestricted-transfer eligibility extends beyond the requested scope. It was researched and excluded. EPP/MSI’s actual official application portal says the 2027 cycle is canceled, overriding recurring annual-window pages.
- Michigan WISE is an explicitly named sophomore programme with a fourth/fifth-semester Winter 2027 focus; SAP’s USA FAQ explicitly targets freshmen/sophomores at typical four-year colleges. Exact semester/remaining-years and local attendance requirements are shown, rather than claiming universal class-year exclusivity.
- Goodrich uses a hard maximum of 31 transferable credits by May 2027 as an early-college gate; it also admits nontraditional early-college applicants. The precise cap is shown without calling it sophomore-only.
- Palmetto’s Class 2027 timeline publishes May 1, 2027, while its current packet says weekend deadlines move to the preceding Friday. Both are disclosed; April 30 is the safe submission date. Montana’s actual award pages explicitly say open despite a generic portal introduction mentioning November 1.
- Cornell and UNCW cached pages lagged their current live pages. Current application-specific links, deadlines and browser observations were used. HKU’s official first-/second-year eligibility is authoritative; broader input options in its form are not treated as proof of third-year eligibility.
- UF’s current form has a clear freshman/sophomore gate but conflicting/invalid prior-research cutoff wording. That ambiguity is disclosed, and an impossible cutoff date was not copied into the active row.
